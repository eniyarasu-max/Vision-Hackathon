01. PAPER PRESENTATION

MAX 3

Present original research and technical papers in technology, engineering, science, or interdisciplinary domains.

Timing: 7 min presentation + 3 min Q&A
Topics: Engineering, Science, AI, Telecom, VLSI
100-Mark Rubric: Technical (25), Innovation (20), Methodology (20), Results (15), Presentation (10), Q&A (10)

02. PROJECT PRESENTATION

MAX 3

Demonstrate innovative engineering solutions across Hardware, Software, AI/ML, IoT, Embedded, and Communication tracks.

Timing: 8 min demo + 2 min Q&A
Requirements: Block diagram, architecture, live demo
Working prototype or live demonstration is strongly encouraged!
Register for Project >
psychology

03. AI SPRINT

MAX 3
bolt PROBLEM STATEMENT GIVEN ON THE SPOT!

Build a polished single-feature AI prototype in a rapid 2-hour sprint using free-tier tools (Gemini, HuggingFace, etc.).

Domains: Chatbots, Classifiers, Automation
Judging: Functionality (40%), Tech Execution (30%), Innovation (15%), Pitch (15%)
Register for AI Sprint >
gavel

04. ELECTROBID

MAX 3

Electronics Auction & Project Challenge. Each team receives 600 virtual credits to bid on essential components.

Auction Rule: Buy at least 1 Sensor + 1 Controller + 1 Output
The Twist: Real-world problem statement revealed post-auction!
Evaluated on prototype function & efficient credit utilization
Register for ElectroBid >

05. LINE FOLLOWER

MAX 3

Autonomous robotics speed and precision arena. Design an autonomous bot capable of mastering curves, sharp bends, and checkpoints.

Track: High-contrast black line on white grid arena
Scoring: Fastest lap time + obstacle/checkpoint clearance
Calibrate your IR sensors & PID algorithms for high speed!
